Sourcing

Where it comes from

1,830m Huila, Colombia

The filter program runs on one lot at a time, bought direct from a single farm above Pitalito. Current pour is a washed caturra, picked in the last two weeks of the harvest window, when the cherry sugars run highest.

We roast in batches of twelve kilos, light enough to keep the acidity intact, and rest each batch nine days before it goes on the bar. Nothing is blended to smooth it out.

Pour overs are brewed at a 1:16 ratio, water just off the boil, through a single paper filter, by hand, every time. The espresso is a separate lot, chosen for body rather than brightness, and pulled on a two-year recipe we haven't felt the need to change.

The room

Hours
Tuesday – Sunday, 7:00 – 15:00
Closed Monday
Address
214 Alder Street
Portland, Oregon

Twelve seats, most of them at the counter. The espresso machine sits at the center of the room, not off to the side, because most of what happens here is worth watching.

Morning light comes in low from the east window until about ten, then the room settles into the kind of even, indirect light that makes it easy to stay through a second cup. We don't play music before nine.
