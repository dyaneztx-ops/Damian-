## Overview
Cypress (often called "Cy-Fair") sits northwest of Houston along US-290. It offers a wide range of homes, from established 1980s–2000s neighborhoods to brand-new master-planned communities.

## Lifestyle
Popular communities like Bridgeland and Towne Lake feature lakes, trails, and resort-style amenities, with improving commutes thanks to the expanded US-290.

## Investor's take
Older Cypress subdivisions can offer solid rental numbers at lower price points. Newer areas tend to have higher MUD taxes, so the numbers need careful review.

## Schools
Cypress is largely served by Cypress-Fairbanks ISD. Verify school assignments directly with the district.
