## Overview
Pearland, in Brazoria County just south of Houston, has grown quickly thanks to its location along TX-288 and easy access to the Texas Medical Center.

## Lifestyle
Communities like Shadow Creek Ranch and Silverlake offer pools, trails, and lakes, with Pearland Town Center for shopping and dining.

## Investor's take
Medical Center proximity supports steady rental demand. Check flood zones and drainage history carefully on any property here, as you should anywhere in the Houston area.

## Schools
Pearland is served by Pearland ISD, Alvin ISD, and Fort Bend ISD depending on location. Verify school assignments directly with the district.
