## Overview
Spring covers a large area of unincorporated north Harris County and southern Montgomery County, offering everything from established neighborhoods to new master-planned communities.

## Lifestyle
Old Town Spring offers local shops, restaurants, and events, and The Woodlands' amenities are a short drive north.

## Investor's take
Spring offers some of the more approachable price points in north Houston, which can make rental numbers work. Check drainage and flood history carefully.

## Schools
Spring is served by several districts, including Spring ISD and Klein ISD. Verify school assignments directly with the district.
