## Overview
Conroe is the county seat of Montgomery County, north of The Woodlands along I-45. It's one of the fastest-growing cities in the region, with new communities alongside older neighborhoods and lake properties.

## Lifestyle
Downtown Conroe has local restaurants, shops, and community events, and Lake Conroe to the northwest draws buyers looking for waterfront homes and weekend boating.

## Investor's take
Lower entry prices than The Woodlands can make rental numbers work. Lake properties are a different animal: check flood history, insurance costs, and any short-term rental rules before you run numbers.

## Schools
Much of Conroe is served by Conroe ISD, with some areas in other districts. Verify school assignments directly with the district.
