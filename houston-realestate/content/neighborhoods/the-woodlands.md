## Overview
The Woodlands is a large master-planned community about 30 miles north of Downtown Houston, organized into villages and known for its wooded setting.

## Lifestyle
The Waterway, Market Street, and the Cynthia Woods Mitchell Pavilion make the town center a destination, and trails connect much of the community.

## Investor's take
Strong demand and higher price points. Rentals can work, but numbers vary widely by village and tax rate, so every deal needs its own analysis.

## Schools
Most of The Woodlands is served by Conroe ISD, with some areas in other districts. Verify school assignments directly with the district.
