## Overview
Missouri City sits in Fort Bend County southwest of Houston, offering established neighborhoods, golf-course communities, and newer master-planned areas like Sienna.

## Lifestyle
Parks, trails, and golf are big here, with Sugar Land's shopping and dining just next door.

## Investor's take
Often a better value than neighboring Sugar Land, which can help rental cash flow. Compare tax rates carefully between subdivisions.

## Schools
Missouri City is served primarily by Fort Bend ISD. Verify school assignments directly with the district.
