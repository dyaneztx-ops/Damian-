## Overview
League City sits in the Houston Bay Area between Houston and Galveston, with easy access to Clear Lake, Kemah, and NASA Johnson Space Center.

## Lifestyle
Boating, marinas, and waterfront dining are a big part of life here, with Galveston beaches a short drive away.

## Investor's take
Bay Area demand is steady, but windstorm and flood insurance can significantly affect your numbers. Always get insurance quotes before you commit.

## Schools
League City is served primarily by Clear Creek ISD. Verify school assignments directly with the district.
