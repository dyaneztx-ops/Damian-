## Overview
River Oaks is an Inner Loop neighborhood west of Downtown, developed in the 1920s and known for large estate homes, mature oaks, and strict deed restrictions that have protected its character for decades.

## Lifestyle
Residents are minutes from Downtown, the Galleria, and Buffalo Bayou Park, with upscale shopping and dining along West Gray and Westheimer.

## Investor's take
This is a land-value market. Lot size, deed restrictions, and teardown potential often matter more than the house itself, so every deal needs careful due diligence on restrictions and comps.

## Schools
River Oaks is served by Houston ISD, and the area also has several private schools nearby. Verify school assignments directly with the district.
