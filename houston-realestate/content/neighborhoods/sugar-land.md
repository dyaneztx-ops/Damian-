## Overview
Sugar Land is one of Fort Bend County's best-known cities, with established master-planned communities like First Colony, Telfair, and Riverstone, and a polished city center.

## Lifestyle
Sugar Land Town Square hosts events, dining, and shopping, and the city has an extensive network of parks and trails.

## Investor's take
Sugar Land tends to hold value well. Rental demand is steady, but entry prices are higher, so cash flow often depends on the specific subdivision and tax rate.

## Schools
Most of Sugar Land is served by Fort Bend ISD. Verify school assignments directly with the district.
