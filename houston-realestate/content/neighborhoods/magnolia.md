## Overview
Magnolia sits in western Montgomery County, northwest of The Woodlands. It has grown quickly as SH 249 and the Grand Parkway made the drive into Houston easier, and it mixes acreage properties with newer subdivisions.

## Lifestyle
Buyers come here for space: bigger lots, room for a shop or animals on acreage tracts, and a quieter pace, with The Woodlands' shopping and dining a short drive east.

## Investor's take
New-construction supply keeps competition for rentals real, so pricing has to be sharp. Acreage and land deals need extra homework: septic, well, utilities, deed restrictions, and floodplain.

## Schools
Much of the area is served by Magnolia ISD, with some addresses in other districts. Verify school assignments directly with the district.
