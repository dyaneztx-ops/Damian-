## Overview
The Heights is one of Houston's oldest planned neighborhoods and still one of its most in-demand. Expect a mix of restored early-1900s bungalows, Victorian homes, and newer townhomes, all inside the 610 Loop and close to Downtown.

## Lifestyle
Tree-lined streets, local boutiques on 19th Street, the Heights Hike & Bike Trail, and a long list of restaurants and patios make this one of the most walkable areas in the city.

## Investor's take
Values here are driven by location and lot size. Renovation potential can be big, but historic district guidelines and older-home systems (foundation, plumbing, electrical) need to be budgeted carefully. I can help you estimate what a project will really cost.

## Schools
The Heights is served primarily by Houston ISD. Boundaries vary by street, so verify assignments directly with the district.
