Texas licensing

Sales agent qualifying education180 hours (six 30-hour courses)
Broker qualifying education270 hours + 4 years active experience in the past 5 years
Sales agent first renewal (SAE)90 hours of Sales Agent Apprentice Education
Continuing education each renewal18 hours, including 4-hr Legal Update I and 4-hr Legal Update II
License term2 years
TREC membership9 members appointed by the Governor: 6 brokers + 3 public
Broker record retention4 years
Governing lawTRELA = Occupations Code Ch. 1101; TREC rules = 22 TAC

Texas contracts & deadlines

Option fee deliveryWithin 3 days of the effective date
Earnest money deliveryWithin 3 days of the effective date, to the escrow agent
Effective dateThe date final acceptance is communicated back to the other party — every deadline runs from it
Seller's Disclosure NoticeTexas Property Code §5.008
Title insurance ratesPromulgated by the Texas Department of Insurance — identical at every title company

Texas property & homestead

Urban homesteadUp to 10 acres
Rural homestead100 acres single adult · 200 acres family
Homestead conveyanceBoth spouses must sign, even if only one is on title
Home equity lending capAll liens combined ≤ 80% of fair market value
Homestead is NOT protected fromProperty taxes · purchase-money loans · home improvement (mechanic's) liens · home equity loans · federal tax liens
State transfer taxNone. Texas has no real property transfer tax and no state income tax

Texas foreclosure & tenancy

Foreclosure sale dayFirst Tuesday of the month, 10 a.m.–4 p.m., county courthouse
Notice of saleAt least 21 days before the sale — posted, filed with the county clerk, and mailed to the debtor
Cure period (residential homestead)Commonly 20 days to cure default before the notice of sale
Tax sale redemption2 years homestead/agricultural · 180 days other property
Property tax calendarAssessed Jan 1 · due Jan 31 · delinquent Feb 1
Security deposit refund30 days after surrender + forwarding address
Re-key security devicesWithin 7 days of a new tenant taking possession

Federal law

Fair housing protected classes7: race, color, religion, national origin, sex, familial status, disability
Fair housing timeline1968 race/color/religion/national origin → 1974 sex → 1988 familial status + disability
Civil Rights Act of 1866Bars all racial discrimination in property — no exemptions
Lead-based paintHousing built before 1978 · buyer gets 10 days to inspect
TILA right of rescission3 business days — refinances of a principal residence, not purchases
Loan EstimateWithin 3 business days of application
Closing DisclosureAt least 3 business days before closing
