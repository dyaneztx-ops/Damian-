Examination Introduction

Texas Real Estate Sales Agent Practice Examination

This practice examination is presented in the same format you will encounter at the testing center: two separately timed sections, one question at a time, with the ability to mark questions for review and return to them before the section ends.

How this compares to the real exam The actual Texas sales agent exam is 125 questions — 85 national (150 minutes) and 40 state (90 minutes) — and requires 56 of 85 national plus 21 of 40 state to pass. This 100-question version keeps the same national-to-state ratio, the same per-question pacing, and the same proportional passing standard, so the experience and the difficulty of the clock match closely.

Rules for this session

1. Each section is timed independently. When a section's time expires, it is submitted automatically.

2. Within a section you may move freely between questions, change answers, and flag questions with Mark for Review.

3. Once you end a section you cannot return to it — exactly as at the testing center.

4. No scoring feedback is shown during the exam. Your score report and full answer rationales appear after Section 2.

5. An on-screen calculator is available at all times, as it is on the real exam.
